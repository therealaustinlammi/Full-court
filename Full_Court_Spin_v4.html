HOW TO PLAY: Spin to land on a random historical NBA team and season. Sign one unique player from the roster, at any position. Choose a slot before signing; natural positions retain full rating while out-of-position assignments lose OVR. Complete your starting five without spending over $100 million in combined original-year nominal salaries. Don't like the roster? Re-spin for $10 million off your remaining budget. Once you sign, the next spin is free. You cannot draft the same player again, even from a different season. Move players between slots after signing, and swap occupied slots freely. Every player gets an estimated 60–99 overall rating based on that season's production, with extra credit for dominant superstar seasons and hand-calibrated 98–99 ratings for selected all-time performances (like 2015–16 Stephen Curry). A hypothetical 82-game win projection rewards higher-rated lineups and bonuses for complementary skills. Empty slots are temporarily modeled as replacement-level starters (74 OVR). Ratings, fit and projected wins are game estimates, not official 2K ratings, Basketball Reference ratings, or historically measured results. Player statistics are per-game averages computed from season totals. Players without a matching salary are not offered, rather than receiving a made-up salary.
Source records: logan-lauton/NBA-Player-HUD (GitHub). Ratings, best-fit scores and win totals are original estimates based on box-score statistics, not Basketball Reference projections.Requires an internet connection on first launch.
